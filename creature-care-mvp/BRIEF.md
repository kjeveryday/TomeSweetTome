## Vision & pillars

A cozy single-creature care prototype: hatch a creature, keep it happy through short daily visits (feed, play, tidy), and watch it grow through three stages over roughly two weeks of real time. Sessions run under five minutes on one device.

Pillars (use as tie-breakers — when this spec is silent, choose the option that best fits all four):

1. **Never punish.** The creature never dies, never looks distressed, always forgives absence.
2. **A gentle daily appointment.** Real-time rhythm with an explicit, designed stopping point.
3. **Small and warm.** Under 5 minutes per visit, icon-first UI for ages 6–12, text ≤ 2 lines anywhere.
4. **No hooks.** No notifications, no monetization, no social, no fail states.

## Locked decisions (changes from v1)

1. **Day boundary**: a "day" is the **local calendar date**. The 3-CP daily cap resets when the date changes; `DayRolledOver` is emitted lazily (on the first open or action of a new date). Casual clock-shifting is tolerated by design; this is not anti-cheat.
2. **Tuning retuned so the visit arc works**: Feed **+45** Fullness · Play **+25** Spirit, **−10** Energy · Tidy **+20** Spirit. At steady state a standard 3-action visit ends **Content** (F45/S45); arrival next day always shows a gentle need (Peckish/Sleepy). **Beaming (both ≥70) is reachable only via extra, 0-CP actions — this is intentional**: caring beyond the requirement makes the creature beam.
3. **Growth pacing**: Stage 2 at **12 CP** (day ~4 of full visits); Stage 3 at **30 CP** (~6 more full-visit days, day ~10 with perfect attendance; ≈ two weeks real-world with normal missed days).
4. **Absence threshold is 36 hours** (measured against `lastSeen`, the wall-clock time of the last persisted event). A punctual daily player who is a few hours late never sees the absence flow. Being 26h late is a normal visit; 40h+ triggers wake-up + gift.
5. **Energy's one job: it gates Play.** Play requires Energy ≥ 10; below that the Play button shows a gentle resting state (dimmed, "zzz"). Energy regenerates 5/h only while the app is closed. Nothing else uses Energy; it is excluded from mood.
6. **The gift is a sticker with a home**: granted stickers stick onto a **sticker shelf** rendered in the scene and persist. `content.json` defines 6 sticker types; grants pick the next unowned one (repeat cycle when all owned).
7. **Mood rule (Fullness and Spirit only — Energy excluded)**: both ≥70 → **Beaming**; both ≥40 → **Content**; otherwise **Peckish** if Fullness < Spirit, else **Sleepy** (ties → Sleepy, so all-zeros shows Sleepy).
8. **Clock rule**: on open, `elapsed = clamp(now − lastSeen, 0, 72h)`. `lastSeen` never moves backward (store `max(lastSeen, now)` on save), so a backward device clock causes no change and no double-drift when the clock returns.
9. **Initial and bounds**: hatch stats F80 / S80 / E100; all stats clamp to 0–100 on every change. Every tuning number lives in `src/content.json` — none in code.
10. **`ResourceGranted`** (external hook, no source built): grants bonus CP that **bypasses the daily cap** and counts toward growth. CP is monotonic; nothing ever removes it.

## Kernel ladder

1. **Hatch & feed.** Tap egg → creature appears with a name and a mood; Feed changes stats/mood with visible feedback. Playable: open, hatch, feed, quit (state persists).
2. **The clock.** Stats drift on real elapsed time at open; daily 3-CP action cap; extra actions still animate and affect stats at 0 CP; tuck-in dims the scene with "See you tomorrow 🌙" (purely theatrical — any action wakes the creature; no locks).
3. **Growth.** CP accumulates to Stage 2 (12) and Stage 3 (30) with a visible form change and a small celebration at each threshold.
4. **Absence & return.** Away ≥36h → on open: wake-up scene, exactly one sticker (never stacks — one per return regardless of length), Fullness and Spirit set to `max(current, 60)`, sticker shelf renders the collection.
5. **Habitat (stretch — DO NOT BUILD).** Explicitly out of scope for this pass.

## Architecture contract

- `GameState` is the single source of truth. **All mutations go through one `applyEvent(state, event)` reducer** (pure function). Systems *emit* events; only the reducer mutates. UI reads state and dispatches intents, never writes state.
- Systems, one responsibility each:
  - **ClockSystem** — computes elapsed time on app open (per decision 8), emits `TimeElapsed` (drift) and `DayRolledOver`.
  - **CareSystem** — validates care intents (Energy gate, action definitions) and emits `CareActionPerformed` (with whether CP was granted per the daily cap).
  - **GrowthSystem** — watches CP after each event, emits `CreatureStateChanged` at thresholds.
  - **Persistence** — serializes `{state, lastSeen}` to localStorage **synchronously after every event**.
- Canonical events: `Hatched`, `TimeElapsed`, `DayRolledOver`, `CareActionPerformed`, `CreatureStateChanged`, `GiftGranted`, `TuckedIn`, `ResourceGranted`. Add further events only if strictly necessary.
- No globals except GameState. No system reads another system's internals.
- Content is data: species, actions, stat values, thresholds, sticker list, and all copy strings live in `src/content.json`.

## Care rules (full spec)

Stats, all 0–100, floor at 0 with no punishment — low stats only change the idle look to sleepy/peckish, never distressed:

- **Fullness** decays 4/h. **Spirit** decays 2/h. **Energy** regenerates 5/h while the app is closed (never during a session).
- Actions: **Feed** +45 Fullness · **Play** +25 Spirit, −10 Energy (requires Energy ≥ 10) · **Tidy** +20 Spirit. The first 3 actions per local calendar day each grant 1 CP; extra actions grant 0 CP but still animate and affect stats. CP never decays.
- Mood is display-only, per decision 7.
- Growth: Stage 2 at 12 CP, Stage 3 at 30 CP.
- Tuck-in: ends the visit ceremonially — dims the scene, says "See you tomorrow 🌙". Nothing is locked; the stop is designed in, not enforced.
- Absence (≥36h): wake-up scene on open, exactly one sticker, Fullness and Spirit = `max(current, 60)` — the adjustment never lowers a stat below its plain-decay value; CP unchanged.

### Edge cases, resolved

1. Device clock moved backward → `elapsed` clamps to 0; nothing changes; `lastSeen` keeps its max so no double-drift later.
2. Forward jump > 72h → treat as 72h.
3. Absence ≥36h → exactly one gift per return; repeated or longer absences never stack gifts.
4. App killed mid-action → state on reopen matches the last persisted event exactly.
5. All three stats at 0 → mood shows **Sleepy** (tie rule), creature naps, Play is unavailable (Energy < 10); one Feed restores the normal idle. No deeper "bad state" exists.

## Worked example (machine-generated — tests MUST reproduce this trace exactly)

Install Tuesday 5pm; full visit (Feed, Play, Tidy) daily at 5pm; skip the weekend.

| Moment | Pre-visit stats → mood | Post-visit stats, CP → mood |
|---|---|---|
| Tue 5pm hatch | F80 S80 E100 → Beaming | F100 S100 E90, 3 CP → Beaming |
| Wed 5pm | F4 S52 E100 → Peckish | F49 S97 E90, 6 CP → Content |
| Thu 5pm | F0 S49 E100 → Peckish | F45 S94 E90, 9 CP → Content |
| Fri 5pm | F0 S46 E100 → Peckish | F45 S91 E90, 12 CP → Content + **Stage 2** |
| Mon 5pm (72h away) | decays to F0 S0 E100 | wake-up: 1 sticker, F60 S60 E100, 12 CP → Content |

Stage 3 (30 CP) lands after 6 more full-visit days (~day 10 of ownership). From a typical close (F45 S91 E90): **+26h** → F0 S39 E100, normal visit, no gift; **+40h** → absence, 1 gift, F60 S60 E100 → Content.

## Tech decisions (locked)

- **Vanilla JavaScript ES modules. Zero dependencies. No build step.** Served statically; tests run with Node's built-in runner.
- Layout:
  ```
  creature-care-mvp/
    index.html          style.css
    src/
      content.json      # ALL tuning numbers, species, stickers, copy strings
      state.js          # initial GameState + applyEvent reducer (pure, no DOM)
      events.js         # event constructors/types
      systems/clock.js  systems/care.js  systems/growth.js  systems/persistence.js
      ui.js             # render from state; icon-first
      app.js            # wiring: open → clock → events → render
    test/               # *.test.mjs, run via `node --test test/`
  ```
- The pure core (`state.js`, `events.js`, `systems/*` except persistence's storage adapter) must not touch the DOM and must take `now` (ms) as a parameter — never call `Date.now()` internally.
- **Debug clock** (needed for acceptance tests): `app.js` uses a single `getNow()` = `Date.now() + offsetMs`, where `offsetMs` persists in localStorage key `creatureCare.debugClockOffsetMs`. URL param sets it: `?clock=+26h`, `?clock=+40h`, `?clock=-5h` (cumulative), `?clock=reset`. This simulates moving the device clock.
- Save: localStorage key `creatureCare.save.v1` holding `{state, lastSeen}` written synchronously after every event.
- UI, ages 6–12, icon-first: one big scene card; the creature is a friendly CSS/SVG blob whose size/shape/color change per stage (per `content.json`; name it from content — default "Pip"); mood shown as the creature's face plus one word; three big buttons (🍎 Feed · 🪀 Play · 🧹 Tidy) + 🌙 Tuck in; small icon meters for the three stats; a star-trail progress row toward the next stage; sticker shelf; hatch = tap the egg (crack animation); welcome-back overlay with gift reveal; gentle CSS animations for every action. No text over two lines.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Fresh install → hatched and first Feed completed within 60 seconds; no tutorial text over two lines.
2. `?clock=+40h` → wake-up scene, exactly 1 sticker, Fullness/Spirit ≥ 60 and never below their plain-decay values. `?clock=+26h` → normal visit, no gift. `?clock=-5h` → no change at all.
3. 3 actions/day for 4 days → Stage 2 fires on day 4 at 12 CP; the event log shows CP monotonically increasing.
4. Kill the app mid-action, reopen → identical state (last persisted event).
5. Grep the source for tuning numbers (45, 25, 20, 12, 30, 70, 40, 36, 60, 4, 2, 5 …) — none outside `content.json`.
6. A complete visit (open → 3 actions → tuck-in) takes under 3 minutes.
7. `node --test test/` is green and includes a test asserting the worked-example trace above **exactly**, plus: day-cap reset at date change, single-gift-per-return, clock clamps (backward and >72h), growth thresholds, Energy gate on Play, `ResourceGranted` bypassing the cap, persistence round-trip with a fake localStorage, and the mood table including the ties→Sleepy rule.

## Scope fence

Refused for MVP: death/sickness/neglect states; push notifications; multiple creatures, breeding, minigames; monetization; accounts or cloud sync; any reading/library integration (the `ResourceGranted` hook is the only concession — accept the event, build no source for it); habitat/decor (stretch, explicitly not this pass); any framework, bundler, or npm dependency.

## User-authorized extension — book creature generator (2026-07-13)

The later user request supersedes only the scope fence's prohibition on book capture. The added prototype stays local and dependency-free: a user selects a barcode photo or types an ISBN, the browser validates a canonical 978/979 book identifier, and `SHA-256("stacklings:v1:" + isbn13)` deterministically selects a constrained creature family, silhouette, palette, markings, idle, voice, quirk, cosmetic rarity, language coat, and publisher birthmark. The photo is decoded on-device and is never stored or uploaded. Native `BarcodeDetector` is best-effort; manual ISBN entry is the permanent accessibility and compatibility fallback.

`CreatureGenerated` is the sole new canonical event. Applying it preserves all care, growth, sticker, daily-cap, and clock state; previous generated identities are retained in `creatureHistory`. Existing `creatureCare.save.v1` saves without the new optional fields remain valid and render the original Pip. Live camera streams, catalog APIs, accounts, library checkout integration, metadata enrichment, copyrighted cover-character imitation, and generative art remain out of scope.
